A Letter to You

Dear Dad,

Today we pause the whole world just to say it plainly: we love you. Not the quiet, ordinary kind of love that goes without saying — the kind we want to shout, today of all days, so it reaches every corner of the year ahead of you.

Fifty-seven years is not a small thing. It is fifty-seven years of a hand that has never let go of you, even when the road bent in ways you didn't choose. We have watched the Lord guide your steps, and we believe He is not done — He is still guiding you, still going ahead of you, still writing more good chapters for your life.

So today we don't just wish you a happy birthday. We wish you more and more: more years, more strength, more laughter around your table, more mornings to wake up to a family that adores you. We wish your road to be smooth, your heart to be light, and your faith to keep carrying you the way it always has.

Go well, Dad. Go far. Godspeed.

With all our love — Your Family
